Contact

Tell us why you're reaching out.

Every route below is answered by a named owner, not a general inbox. Expected response time: 3–5 working days.

Routes

  • Partnerships

    Institutional, in-kind, and program partnerships — see also Partner / Support

  • Media

    Press enquiries, interviews, and the media kit

  • Events & speaking

    Appearances, panels, and speaking requests

  • Foundation & program

    Questions about active or historical programs — see Our Work

  • Safeguarding

    A sensitive concern about a program or participant — see the Safeguarding & Contact Policy

  • General

    Anything else, including a correction to the Impact Archive

Safeguarding note: if your message concerns a child or a vulnerable person's safety, say so in the enquiry type below — it is routed with priority and handled under the Foundation's safeguarding policy, not as a general enquiry.
