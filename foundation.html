The Foundation

An institution built to outlast any single moment of goodwill.

The Achai Wiir Foundation carries Achai Wiir's public work forward across South Sudan — disability inclusion, child welfare, and emergency relief, delivered in specific places with a source attached.

01Mission & geography

Working copy — pending Foundation approval: The Achai Wiir Foundation exists to deliver dignity-centred support — mobility, welfare, and emergency relief — to communities across South Sudan, with a particular commitment to locations that receive comparatively little national or international attention.

Its confirmed geographic footprint, from reported and Foundation-documented activity to date, spans Juba, Aweil, Wau, and Mangalla. This is described as current footprint, not an exhaustive service area — the Foundation's stated ambition is South Sudan-wide, but that ambition is working copy until further activity is confirmed and sourced.

02Origin & current status

The Achai Wiir Foundation was founded by Achai Wiir. A founding year, legal form, and registration status are not part of the source material this site was built from, so none is stated here — an honest “pending documentation” is preferable to a guess presented as fact. The About Achai page's milestone timeline lists the founding as a dated entry once that date is confirmed.

What can be stated with a source attached is the Foundation's activity record: three independently reported distributions and initiatives between 2021 and 2023, detailed in full on the Impact Archive, plus two further locations documented only in the Foundation's own channels and labelled accordingly.

What the Foundation prioritises.

01

Disability inclusion & mobility Working

Mobility aids and access support for persons with disabilities, including the Aweil and Wau distributions.

Children & community care Working

Food, essential items, and welfare support for children and community centres, including Juba's orphanage network.

03

Emergency & relief support Working

Support for internally displaced people and communities in crisis, delivered with local and institutional partners.

04

Dignity, access & pathways to opportunity Working

Partnership-led interventions — including the 2021 debt-clearance initiative — that restore access and agency.

04How the Foundation works

Selection, delivery, partners, follow-up.

Working copy — a plausible, generic description of process. No Foundation operating manual or standard-operating-procedure document was supplied for this build; the steps below should be reviewed and corrected by the Foundation before publication.

01

Identifying need

Locations and groups are identified through community contacts, local partners, and direct outreach — prioritising places with less existing support.

02

Delivery

Goods and support are delivered directly, often alongside a local or institutional partner named at the time — as with the South Sudan Islamic Council in 2021.

03

Partnership

Where a partner is involved, credit is shared explicitly rather than folded into a single-actor narrative.

04

Follow-up & record

Outcomes are intended to be documented and, where possible, independently reported — the standard this Impact Archive tries to hold every entry to.

05Governance & leadership

Information being updated.

No board, staff, or leadership-team names or titles beyond Achai Wiir as founder were supplied for this build. Rather than invent an organisational chart, this section will list confirmed governance and leadership detail once the Foundation provides it.

06Safeguarding & accountability

A clear route for a sensitive concern.

Programs described on this site involve children and people in vulnerable circumstances. The Foundation's approach to consent, identification, and reporting a concern is set out on the dedicated Safeguarding & Contact Policy page — including how to raise a concern directly.

07Documents
