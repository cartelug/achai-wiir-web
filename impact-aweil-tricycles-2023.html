98 tricycles reach persons with disabilities in Aweil.

Reported by Radio Tamazuj, January 2023 — the Foundation's most strongly evidenced single record to date.

Reported by publisher
Illustrative reference image (Wau, Jul 2023) — not from the Aweil event · rights clearance pending

Location & date range

Aweil, Northern Bahr el Ghazal — reported January 2023.

Purpose & delivery summary

A distribution event providing tricycles to persons with disabilities, alongside food packages for blind and deaf community groups in the same area. This is presented as a single reported event, not an ongoing program with a defined future schedule.

Confirmed outputs

  • 98 tricycles distributed to persons with disabilities.
  • Food packages distributed to blind and deaf community groups.

An earlier planning reference used the figure “100 motorised wheelchairs.” The published report uses “98 tricycles” — this record uses the reported figure and terminology and states the discrepancy rather than reconciling it unilaterally.

Partners & roles

Radio Tamazuj is the source reporting this event, not a delivery partner — the two should not be conflated. No third-party implementation or funding partner is named in the source; partner detail is unknown/pending.
