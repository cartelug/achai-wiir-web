Safeguarding & contact policy

Working draft, last updated 3 September 2026 — pending formal legal review.

Why this page exists

Several programs described on this site involve children and people in vulnerable circumstances — including the Juba orphanage-centre support and the Aweil and Wau disability-inclusion distributions. This page states the rules this site holds itself to when writing about them, and how to raise a concern.

How children are represented

  • No child is identified by full name, specific medical or disability condition, or precise home location without documented guardian consent on file.
  • Photography involving children is treated as reference-only, pending the same clearance standard as every other image on this site.
  • Field notes involving children (see Stories) are written as composite, illustrative accounts rather than attributed to a real, identifiable child.

How vulnerable adults are represented

The July 2021 debt-clearance initiative involved people released from detention. This site does not name or identify any of the eight individuals involved, and does not describe their personal or financial circumstances — see the related field note for the reasoning.

Reporting a concern

If you have a safeguarding concern about a Foundation program, a participant, or content on this site, use the Safeguarding route on the Contact page. Concerns marked Safeguarding are treated as priority and are not handled as general enquiries.

Contact policy, generally

This site does not publish a personal phone number, email address, or social handle for Achai Wiir or Foundation staff — public profiles were found to disagree with each other, and this site would rather route enquiries by role, through Contact, than publish an address that may not be accurate or current.
