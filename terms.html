What this site is

This is a design and content concept for the eventual official site of Achai Wiir and the Achai Wiir Foundation. Content marked “working copy” is a draft pending approval; photography marked reference-only is not cleared for public use; figures without a cited source should not be treated as confirmed.

No donation or payment functionality

This site does not process donations, payments, or any financial transaction. Any reference to “giving” or “support” routes to a conversation via the Contact or Partner pages, not a payment flow.

External links

This site links to independent news coverage (Eye Radio, Radio Tamazuj) and to the Foundation's own social media posts as source material. Those sites are operated independently, and this site is not responsible for their content or availability.

No warranty
