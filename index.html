03Leadership

Conviction, in the first person.

Working copy — pending Achai's and the Foundation's approval:

Achai Wiir has built a public voice around a simple conviction: that dignity is not something communities wait for, but something leadership delivers in specific, visible, and accountable ways. That conviction sits behind the Foundation that carries her name — an institution built to outlast any single moment of goodwill.

“The measure of the work is not the announcement. It is what changed, for whom, and how we can show it.”

05Working categories — pending Foundation confirmation

What the Foundation does.

01

Disability inclusion & mobility Working

Mobility aids and access support for persons with disabilities, including the Aweil and Wau distributions.

02

Children & community care Working

Food, essential items, and welfare support for children and community centres, including Juba's orphanage network.

03

Emergency & relief support Working

Support for internally displaced people and communities in crisis, delivered with local and institutional partners.

04

Dignity, access & pathways to opportunity Working

Partnership-led interventions — including the 2021 debt-clearance initiative — that restore access and agency.
