Accessibility statement

Working draft, last updated 3 September 2026 — pending formal legal review.

Our aim

This site is built with accessibility as a baseline requirement, not an afterthought: semantic HTML landmarks and headings; visible focus states on every interactive element; a skip-to-content link; alt text on every image; decorative elements (the impact map, the preloader's engraved portrait) marked as such for assistive technology, with their information also available as plain text nearby.

Motion & animation

Every scroll reveal, image wipe, and the homepage preloader respect the operating system's prefers-reduced-motion setting — when it's set to reduce, this site shows all content immediately, fully readable, with no animation. The same content and navigation are also fully available if a script fails to load or is blocked, including on a slow or metered connection.

Colour & contrast

Body text uses Ink (#161B20) or Dark Bronze on light backgrounds, chosen to meet contrast targets; Heritage Gold is intentionally reserved for rules, icons, and large display accents rather than body text, since it does not meet contrast requirements at small sizes on light backgrounds.

Known gaps

This is a working concept build, not a certified WCAG-conformant product. A full accessibility audit — including with screen-reader users and keyboard-only navigation testing across every page — has not yet been performed and should happen before public launch.

Reporting an issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, use the General route on Contact to report it.
