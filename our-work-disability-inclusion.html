Disability inclusion & mobility.

Mobility aids and access support for persons with disabilities — the Foundation's most strongly evidenced program category to date.

Purpose

Working copy: to reduce mobility and access barriers for persons with disabilities in South Sudan through direct distribution of mobility aids and, where reported, complementary support for other disability groups in the same community.

Who it serves & where

Persons with disabilities in Aweil received tricycles in January 2023; blind and deaf community groups in the same area received food packages as part of the same reported activity. A separate mobility-aid distribution event is documented by Foundation reference photography in Wau, July 2023, without a published article or figure attached.

Current status & date range

Active reported period: January 2023 (Aweil) through July 2023 (Wau, records pending). No activity in this category is confirmed beyond that range as of this writing.

Approach & partners

Working copy — pending Foundation confirmation: distribution is understood to be direct, at community events, rather than through an intermediary organisation. No third-party implementation partner is named in the Aweil source; partners for the Wau event are not yet documented.

Verified metrics

98 tricycles distributed to persons with disabilities in Aweil, plus food packages for blind and deaf community groups — reported by Radio Tamazuj, January 2023. An earlier planning reference used the figure “100 motorised wheelchairs”; this site uses the published, reported figure and terminology (98 tricycles) and notes the discrepancy rather than reconciling it unilaterally.

Source & verification
Radio Tamazuj — Achai Wiir Foundation distributes tricycles to PWDs in Aweil ↗
