Partner / Support

Build lasting impact with the Foundation.

Every route below reaches a named owner at the Foundation — no undifferentiated inbox, and no payment flow until a legal recipient and provider are verified.

01What partnership can enable

Working copy: partnership with the Achai Wiir Foundation can mean funding or in-kind support for a specific, sourced program category; joint delivery alongside an institutional partner, in the pattern already set by the 2021 debt-clearance initiative; or media and research collaboration that helps verify and extend the Impact Archive itself. What it cannot yet mean is a direct donation transaction — see “Discuss support” below.

03Partnership types

Five ways in.

Program partnership

Co-fund or co-deliver one of the four working categories above.

In-kind support

Goods, logistics, or transport for a specific reported or planned distribution.

Institutional partnership

NGO, embassy, or government collaboration on joint delivery, in the pattern of the 2021 debt-clearance initiative.

Research & media

Help verify, document, or extend the Impact Archive itself — fact-checking, photography, or field reporting.

Event & speaking

Host or co-host an appearance built around a specific, sourced program.

04What the Foundation provides

Working copy: a named point of contact for the partnership's duration; documentation of the specific activity a partnership supports, added to the Impact Archive with a source; and a reporting approach that favours a small number of independently verifiable outcomes over broad, unsourced claims.

05Due diligence — stated plainly

What isn't yet documented.

A prospective institutional partner will reasonably ask for registration and governance documentation. As of this writing, the Foundation's registration status, legal form, and board composition are not documented for publication — see the Foundation page for the full, honest status. This site would rather say so than paper over it with invented compliance material; a serious partner should ask for this directly as part of any real due-diligence process.
